Event Security Service Consulting in Miami & South Florida

The best security and safety plan for your event, so your patrons have a great experience in a safe environment.

An event should feel welcoming to the people who attend it and safe at the same time. S3 Consulting builds the best security and safety plan for your event, so your patrons have a great experience in a safe environment.

We plan around your venue, your crowd and the risks you actually face. That means a plan that keeps patrons safe and the experience smooth, with the right structure, the right people and the right training, rather than the biggest plan or the longest invoice.

Our methods draw on American, Israeli and British security practice: clear, documented plans and procedures; a proactive mindset with layered perimeters, early detection and alert, trained people; and proportionate, risk-based protection that deters, detects, delays and responds.

We tell you what you need and what you don't, and we work within your budget. When you need people on post, we provide security personnel as well, or help you contract the right security company for your event.

We work with events and venues, nonprofits and community organizations across South Florida. Our personnel have extensive experience in the nonprofit and healthcare sectors.
