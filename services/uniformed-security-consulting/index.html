Uniformed Security Consulting in Miami & South Florida

Security personnel for your site, help contracting the best security company, and customized training for your current staff.

Sometimes the right answer is people on post. S3 Consulting provides security personnel for your site, helps you contract the best security company for your needs, and delivers customized training for your current staff.

We start with what you actually need. Some sites need officers. Some need a better contract with the company they already use. Some need their own staff trained to a higher standard. We tell you which one fits, and we do not overpromise.

Our approach is grounded in American, Israeli and British security practice, with one shared principle: trained, alert people are the most important part of any system. Clear procedures, a proactive mindset and proportionate, risk-based protection guide how personnel are placed and trained.

We work with organizations across South Florida, including nonprofits, healthcare settings, events and venues, and government and security teams. Our personnel have extensive experience in the nonprofit and healthcare sectors.

Uniformed security consulting fits naturally with our other services: SOP creation and testing so officers have procedures that work, and advanced training for the people on post.
