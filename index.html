Our background

Three security traditions. One foundation.

Our methods are grounded in American, Israeli and British security theory and practice. We take the strongest structures from each and use them as the foundation for a capable team and well-protected assets.

US

American

Structure and standards: documented plans, clear procedures, and layered physical and electronic security that can be tested and measured.

IL

Israeli

A proactive, threat-driven mindset: layered perimeters, early detection, and trained, alert people as the most important part of any system.

UK

British

Proportionate, risk-based protective security: deter, detect, delay and respond, matched to what an organization really needs.
